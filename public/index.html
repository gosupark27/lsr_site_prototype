Our Story

About Lee's

Family-owned craftsmanship and personal service have been at the heart of Lee's Shoe & Luggage Repair since 1994.

Lee's Shoe & Luggage Repair is a family-owned repair shop serving Peoria and the West Valley since 1994. For more than three decades, we've helped customers extend the life of their shoes, boots, handbags, luggage, and leather goods through skilled repair and restoration.

Our work is rooted in a family tradition of hands-on craftsmanship. We believe many well-made items are worth repairing rather than replacing, and we take pride in finding practical solutions for the things our customers value.

Whether it's a favorite pair of boots, a well-used handbag, or luggage that has traveled for years, our goal remains simple: provide quality workmanship, straightforward service, and repairs made to last.
